01

The problem

Every year, disasters kill thousands. Not because we can’t predict them. Because we don’t know what to do next.

⚠️

Warnings exist, coordination doesn’t

Alerts are issued, but no structured response follows: who moves what, where, by when.

📊

Data is available, insights aren’t

Decades of disaster records and live feeds sit in separate places, unused when decisions are made.

👥

People want to help, no system connects them

Volunteers, transporters and NGOs go unmatched to the needs that matter most.

02

What is DisasterIQ

One platform, from data to done.

DisasterIQ is a Disaster Management Analytics + Prediction + Coordination platform that turns raw disaster data into structured, trackable emergency response — for earthquakes, floods and cyclones in India.

  1. 🛰️Live Data
  2. 🔮Prediction

04

The Action Hub

Where prediction becomes action.

The coordination loop that most dashboards stop short of: every step is recorded and every open screen updates live.

  1. 🧠DisasterIQ
  2. 📡Detects riskLevel 2 flood model and hazard indices
  3. 📍Identifies vulnerable areashigh and critical districts and states
  4. ✨Gemini AI creates the action plana needs list sized to the population
  5. 🔎Finds available respondersSmart Match: availability, location, role, workload
  6. 📨Assigns tasksnumbered from #1001
  7. 🙌PEOPLE ACT
  8. 📱People update the appstatus, photo proof, location, notes
  9. ✅DisasterIQ tracks completion
  10. 📊Readiness score updates

05

Tech stack

Built on open data and a modern stack.

Every number traces to a public source; anything a source does not record is shown as unavailable, never estimated.

Data & APIs

  • 📚
    EM-DAT via Our World in DataHistorical impact: deaths, affected, economic loss
  • 🏔️
    USGSEarthquake catalogue (M6+ since 1900) and live feed
  • 🌀
    NOAA IBTrACSCyclone best tracks since 1980
  • 🌊
    India Flood Inventory (IIT Delhi)Flood events 1967–2023 by district, the flood model’s label
  • 🛰️
    NASA POWER · NASA EONETRainfall and soil moisture since 1981; live natural events
  • 🚨
    GDACSGlobal disaster alerts and cyclone tracks
  • 🌦️
    Open-MeteoLive forecast, temperature normals, sea surface, geocoding
  • ✨
    Google Gemini 2.5 FlashRisk narratives and action-plan needs lists

Platform

  • ⚛️
    React 19 + ViteSingle-page app, code-split per tab
  • 📈
    RechartsEvery chart, with a data table and CSV export
  • 🗺️
    LeafletSatellite world map, district risk map, impact rings
  • 🐍
    FastAPI (Python)Analytics, risk and recommendation APIs; Gemini key stays server-side
  • 🧮
    pandas + scikit-learnData pipeline and the district flood model
  • 🔥
    Firebase Cloud FirestoreLive Action Hub data: responders, plans, tasks, updates
  • 🧪
    Vitest + pytestAutomated tests on both frontend and backend
  • ☁️
    RenderHosting for the web app and the API
